## ✍️ Description & Execution
- Called when doing [[Dame]] or on the 123 of the [[Dile Que No]]
### Leader
- 123 - [[Dile Que No]]
- 5 - Raise the left hand, follower goes under it
- 67 - Walk to the partner on your left
- 123 - Walking to the other partner and do [[Dile Que No]] (when you get there)
	- You will not be on time to start it but connect on 3
- 567 - Finish
### Follower
- After going under the leaders hand you start [[Dile Que No]] on your own and the leader will catch up on 3
