## ✍️ Description & Execution
- *Regressa* is the call to change from por Arriba to por Abajo
### Leader
#### Sombrero por Arriba
- 123 567 - [[Sombrero]]
	- At the end you need to be inside the circle and follower outside
- Walk until something else is called
	- Walk direction is counter clockwise
#### Sombrero por Abajo
- 123 567 - Do [[Coca-Cola]] like turn, keeping the hands
	- You should end up in *Reverse* [[Sombrero]] (follower is on my left)
	- Leader still inside the circle but now facing the other direction
- Walk until something else is called
	- Walk direction is clockwise
